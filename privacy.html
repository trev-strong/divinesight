Divine Sight Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Divine Sight is a numerology and geomancy app made by Trevor Strong. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: the app doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

To make readings, the app asks for names and birthdates. These, along with any places you save on the map and any questions you type into the geomantic shield, are stored only on your device. They are used to calculate your readings on the device and are never sent to us or anyone else. The home screen and lock screen widgets read the selected profile from the same on-device storage.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only to center the geomancy map on where you are. Your location is used on your device and is not sent to us or stored by us. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings, and the map still works.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reading notification, the app schedules it on your device. No information is sent to a server to do this.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is active so it can unlock Premium features. Apple's own handling of your purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier.

Sharing

When you share a reading, the app creates an image on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes; nothing is shared unless you do it.

Children

Divine Sight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

Because your information is stored only on your device, you can delete it by deleting profiles in the app or by deleting the app.

Readings

Readings are for reflection and entertainment. They are not advice and do not predict the future.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
